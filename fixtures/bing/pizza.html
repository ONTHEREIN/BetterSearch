    To make pizza, mix a simple yeast dough, let it rise, shape it, add tomato sauce and cheese, then bake in a very hot oven (typically 450–500°F / 230–260°C). This process appears consistently across reliable recipes such as those from Indian Healthy Recipes, Tasty, BBC Good Food, and WikiHow.

    Essential Steps (Beginner-Friendly)

    1. Make the Dough

    Most recipes use flour, warm water, yeast, salt, and a little oil.

    • Activate yeast in warm water with sugar until foamy. Swasthi's Recipes
    • Mix with flour, salt, and oil to form a soft dough.
    • Knead 5–15 minutes until smooth and elastic. Tasty
    • Let rise 1–4 hours (or longer for cold fermentation). thepizzaplanet.com

    2. Prepare the Sauce

    A simple tomato sauce works best:

    • Cook tomatoes with garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper until thickened. Swasthi's Recipes+1
    • Alternatively, blend canned tomatoes with salt for a quick sauce. Tasty

    3. Shape the Dough

    • Punch down risen dough and divide into portions.
    • Stretch or roll into thin rounds; keep edges slightly thicker. Swasthi's Recipes
    • If dough resists stretching, let it rest 10–15 minutes. thepizzaplanet.com

    4. Assemble the Pizza

    • Spread a thin layer of sauce.
    • Add mozzarella or other cheeses.
    • Add toppings sparingly to avoid sogginess. thepizzaplanet.com

    5. Bake

    • Preheat oven to its highest safe temperature (450–500°F / 230–260°C). Tasty+1
    • Use a preheated pizza stone, steel, or baking sheet for best results. Tasty+1
    • Bake 10–15 minutes until crust is crisp and cheese is golden. Swasthi's Recipes

    Tips for Better Pizza

    • Preheat oven and stone/steel for at least 25–45 minutes. Swasthi's Recipes+1
    • Use bread flour or “00” flour depending on style. thepizzaplanet.com
    • Keep toppings light for a crisp center.
    • A pizza steel improves browning in home ovens. completelydelicious.com

    Quick Ingredient List (Typical)

    • Flour, warm water, yeast, salt, olive oil
    • Tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
    • Mozzarella cheese
    • Optional toppings: pepperoni, vegetables, basil, mushrooms, onions, etc. wikihow.com

    Homemade pizza relies on a hot oven, well-risen dough, and balanced toppings to achieve a crisp, flavorful crust.

    Pizza Dough

    1-1/2 cups (375 ml) warm water
    1 package or 2-1/4 tsp (11.25 ml) active dry yeast
    3-1/2 cups (875 ml) bread flour
    2 Tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
    2 tsp (10 ml) salt

    Pizza Sauce

    1 Tbsp (15 ml) olive oil
    2 tsp (10 ml) minced garlic
    1/4 cup (60 ml) minced sweet onion
    1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) dried oregano
    1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) dried basil
    1 lb (450 g) diced fresh tomatoes
