    Making a great homemade pizza involves three main parts: preparing the dough, assembling the toppings, and baking at a high temperature. 
    1. Make the Pizza Dough
    You can use a quick recipe or let the dough cold-ferment in the fridge for extra flavor. 
    • Mix ingredients: Combine warm water (around 110°F), a packet of yeast, a pinch of sugar, flour, olive oil, and salt.
    • Knead: Knead the dough for 5 to 7 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
    • Rise: Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours until doubled in size.
    • Shape: Punch down the dough and divide it into balls. On a floured surface, stretch the dough gently with your hands or knuckles into a 12-to-16-inch round circle, leaving the outer edge slightly thicker for the crust. 
    For a beginner-friendly guide, try the Homemade Pizza Crust Recipe from Sally's Baking Addiction. 
    2. Prepare the Sauce and Toppings
    • Sauce: Use crushed San Marzano or whole peeled tomatoes mixed with a pinch of salt, a touch of sugar, oregano, and basil. Do not cook the sauce beforehand; it will cook on the pizza.
    • Cheese: Low-moisture mozzarella works best for a standard home oven because it melts evenly without making the crust soggy. You can mix in a little Parmesan or Pecorino for extra flavor.
    • Assemble: Lay your stretched dough on parchment paper or a semolina-dusted pizza peel. Spread a thin layer of sauce, add your cheese, and keep extra toppings light so the crust stays crisp. 
    3. Bake the Pizza
    • Preheat: Heat your oven as hot as it will go (usually between 450°F and 550°F). If you have a pizza steel or pizza stone, preheat it inside the oven for 30 to 45 minutes.
    • Bake: Transfer the pizza onto the hot steel or baking sheet. Bake for 6 to 12 minutes (depending on your oven's top heat) until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
    • Finish: Rest the pizza on a wire rack for 1 to 2 minutes, then drizzle with extra virgin olive oil or top with fresh basil.
